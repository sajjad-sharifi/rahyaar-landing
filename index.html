Why Rahyaar

Built around who gets helped

A few things we hold to, on every match.

Private by default

You do not need to use your real name, and you never have to share anything that identifies you.

Free, always

No fees and no subscriptions. All you need is an email so a mentor can reach you.

Dari and English

Ask for the language you are most comfortable in, and we match a mentor who speaks it.

Vetted mentors

Every mentor is verified with a LinkedIn profile and five endorsers before any match is made.

The journeys we support

Wherever you are on the road

Tell us your goal and we match you with a mentor who has made the same move.

  • → School to university
  • → Undergraduate to master's or work
  • → Master's to PhD or work
  • → Changing careers
  • → Skilled migration
Your privacy comes first

This is a space built by and for the Hazara community, and open to mentors of any background who want to help. What you share is up to you, and it stays private.
